## Outline

1. **H1: USS Arizona Memorial — Oahu Visitor Guide**
2. **Intro** — The Arizona Memorial stands over the sunken USS Arizona, where 1,177 crew members lost their lives on Dec 7, 1941. A solemn, essential visit.
3. **History** — Brief, respectful overview: the attack on Pearl Harbor, the sinking of the Arizona, construction of the memorial (built 1962)
4. **Visiting the Memorial**
   - Access only by Navy shuttle boat from Pearl Harbor Visitor Center
   - Tour includes: 23-min documentary, boat ride, 15 min on the memorial
   - Total time: 75 minutes recommended
5. **Tickets: Two Paths**
   - **Reserved**: recreation.gov, released 8 weeks ahead, free, limited
   - **Walk-up**: Same-day tickets at 7am (limited, queue early)
   - Special: Golden Age/Hawaii resident passes
6. **What You'll See**
   - The shrine room (names of the fallen)
   - The assembly area
   - Oil droplets ("black tears of the Arizona") still rising from the wreck
   - View of USS Missouri across the harbor
7. **Visitor Tips**
   - No bags allowed (lockers available for $8)
   - Arrive 30 min early
   - Quiet, respectful attire
   - Best light for photos: 9-11am
8. **Pearl Harbor Complex** — Arizona Memorial + USS Missouri + USS Bowfin + Aviation Museum = full day
9. **FAQ** — Can I visit without a reservation? Is there parking? Can I bring my phone?
