## Outline

1. **H1: Jurassic Park on Oahu — Kualoa Ranch Filming Location Guide**
2. **Intro** — Kualoa Ranch has been the backdrop for Jurassic Park, Jurassic World, Jumanji, Kong: Skull Island, and more. This 4,000-acre nature reserve is a working ranch AND movie studio.
3. **Movie History at Kualoa**
   - Jurassic Park (1993): The gallimimus chase field, the famous "they're flocks!" scene
   - Jurassic World (2015): Indominus Rex paddock, gyrosphere valley
   - Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom: More Kualoa scenes
   - Other films: 50 Shades of Grey, Jumanji, Godzilla, Lost (TV)
4. **Tours Available**
   - **Movie Sites Tour** (1.5 hrs, bus) — Most popular, covers all filming locations, guide points out scenes
   - **ATV Tour** (2 hrs) — Drive through valleys, see sets up close
   - **E-Bike Tour** (2 hrs) — Quiet, electric, more intimate
   - **Horseback Tour** (1-2 hrs) — Ranch experience
   - **Jurassic Adventure Tour** (3 hrs) — Combo of movie sites + jungle expedition
5. **Prices & Booking**
   - Adult pricing range: $50-150 depending on tour type
   - Book online (kualoa.com), tours sell out especially in summer
   - Transportation: Rental car (30 min from Waikiki) or shuttle included with some tours
6. **Tips for Visitors**
   - Book 1+ week ahead in summer
   - Morning tours have better light for photos
   - Closed-toe shoes for ATV/horse
   - Bring binoculars for spotting movie locations
7. **Kualoa + Windward Coast Day**
   - Morning: Kualoa Ranch movie tour
   - Midday: Lunch in Kailua or Kaneohe
   - Afternoon: Kayak with Active Oahu (Kaneohe Sandbar, Chinaman's Hat, or Mokulua Islands)
   - Active Oahu is minutes from Kualoa Ranch — perfect pairing
8. **FAQ** — Can you see dinosaurs? (No, but the sets are incredible), How far from Waikiki? (40 min), Is it worth it? (Yes)
