2. **Intro** — Pearl Harbor is Oahu's most-visited attraction. Whether you book a guided tour or go independently, here's everything you need.
3. **Guided Tour Options**
   - Tour operators that pick up from Waikiki hotels
   - Typical inclusions: transportation, Arizona Memorial ticket, guide
   - Price range: $50-120/person
   - What to look for: early pickup (beat crowds), small groups, included lunch
4. **DIY (Self-Guided) Option**
   - Getting there: Drive ($5 parking) or bus (TheBus #20/#42 from Waikiki)
   - Total DIY cost: $0 ticket + transportation
   - Best for: visitors with rental cars, flexible schedules
5. **Pearl Harbor Attractions Breakdown**
   - Arizona Memorial (75 min, free with reservation)
   - USS Missouri (2-3 hours, $35)
   - USS Bowfin Submarine (1 hour, $20)
   - Pearl Harbor Aviation Museum (2 hours, $25)
6. **Half-Day vs Full-Day Planning**
   - Half-day: Arizona Memorial only
   - Full-day: Arizona Memorial + USS Missouri
   - Extended: Add Bowfin + Aviation Museum
7. **Tips for Booking**
   - Book Arizona Memorial tickets at recreation.gov as early as possible
   - Guided tours handle the reservation — convenient but more expensive
   - Cancelation policies vary
