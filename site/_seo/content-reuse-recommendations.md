## 2. What to Rewrite or Retire (Contradictions & Outdated Copy)

We must retire or completely rewrite several sections of the drafts due to changes in Active Oahu Tours' business model and operations.

### A. The "On-Site Delivery" Model (Retire & Rewrite)
*   **The Contradiction:** All 7 documents describe a model where AOT guides meet guests at the beach or local landmarks (Keneke's Grill in Punaluʻu, Kualoa Regional Park bus stop, or Kawela Bay roadside) to deliver kayaks, load them, and pick them up.
*   **The Correction:** The live site operates on a **Storefront Pickup** model. Guests must check-in at the Kailua storefront (134B Hamakua Dr), load the kayaks onto their own 4-door vehicle using provided foam pads and straps, and drive themselves to the launch spots (35-45 minutes away).
*   **Action:**
    *   Retire all references to "guides in white trucks," meeting at Keneke's Grill for delivery, and direct-to-beach kayak handoffs.
    *   Rewrite all logistics pages to emphasize the storefront check-in process, vehicle requirements (must have a 4-door vehicle or roof racks), and the drive times.

### B. Tour Durations (Align with Live Site)
*   **The Contradiction:** Abigail's Chinaman's Hat drafts refer to a "3 hour experience" in the FareHarbor notes.
*   **The Correction:** The current live rental page lists the duration as "4 hours" (which includes pickup, transport, launch, paddle, and return).
*   **Action:** Keep the 4-hour duration standard across all marketing materials to avoid setting expectations for short windows.

## 3. Missing Topics & Product Expansion Opportunities (Gaps)

The biggest gap revealed is that **Kawela Bay does not have an active booking/transactional page** on `activeoahutours.com`—only a blog post. This is a high-value opportunity.

### Recommendation: Create a new Kawela Bay Self-Guided Tour & Rental Page
Use Abigail's content drafts (Docs 6 and 7) to launch a new transactional product page `/activities/kawela-bay-self-guided-kayak-tour/`.

#### Key Sections to Include on the New Page:
1.  **Product Copy:** Describe Kawela Bay as a protected, crescent-shaped North Shore sanctuary safe for families and children year-round, even when winter swells hit the rest of the North Shore.
2.  **Film History Section (High SEO Search Intent):** Highlight that the bay served as the backdrop for *The Hunger Games: Catching Fire* (the arena beach), *Lost*, *Pirates of the Caribbean*, and *Soul Surfer*.
3.  **WWII Pillbox Hike (Protection Point):** Describe the 10-minute walk along the dirt trail to the Protection Point bunker. Detail the history of the civilian defense troops in the 1940s protecting the Kahuku Air Corps Airfield (B-17/B-24 base) and the Pearl Harbor radio transmissions.
4.  **Hidden Freshwater Spring:** Describe the cool freshwater spring on the southwest end of the bay and explain the cultural significance of *wai* (fresh water) to the native Hawaiians.
5.  **Shallow Reef Safety Warning (Essential Navigation):** Instruct paddlers to launch on the left side of the bay and avoid the right side, which is shallow and covered in fragile, sharp coral reefs.
6.  **Equipment Inclusions (The Kayak Trolley):** Emphasize that because Kawela Bay requires a short walk through the jungle path from the highway parking, AOT includes a **kayak trolley/cart** in the rental package to make wheeling the kayak to the beach effortless.
7.  **Local Food Pairings:** Recommend grabbing food at the Kahuku Food Trucks or dessert at Ted's Bakery (Chocolate Haupia Pie) after the tour.
