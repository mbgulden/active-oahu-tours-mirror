## 1. Core Objectives & Approach

The goal of this initiative is to surface every strategic question that Michael Gulden (owner of Active Oahu Tours) should ask about his business and website. These questions are designed to lead to better rankings, improved cashflow, higher guest value, and a more authentic/authoritative online presence. 

This is a **meta-audit**: it does not answer the questions directly, but rather identifies and prioritizes them so they can be answered in future work.

Our approach consists of:
1. **Context Extraction**: Synthesize technical audits (149 missing schema pages, 7 orphan pages), competitor analysis (AOT vs. KBA vs. Surf n Sea), content-reuse plans (Abigail's drafts on Chinaman's Hat, Kahana River, and Kawela Bay), and voice guidelines (from the brand origin and tour guides interviews).
2. **Analysis of GSC & GA4 Logs**: Cross-reference high exits and high impression/low CTR queries to pinpoint conversion leaks and traffic drops.
3. **Structured Categorization**: Categorize questions into four distinct performance outcomes:
   - **Ranking (SEO Gaps)**: Technical, content, authority, and competitive opportunities.
   - **Cashflow (Revenue Gaps)**: Conversion, pricing/positioning, upselling, and retention.
   - **Guest Value (Experience Gaps)**: Information, trust, logistics, and post-booking support.
   - **Authority & Authenticity (EEAT Gaps)**: Local expertise, cultural respect, originality, and voice consistency.
4. **Data Gaps Definition**: Identify what tracking or intelligence is currently missing.
5. **Master Prioritization Matrix**: Map all questions into a priority matrix (P0, P1, P2) linking each to its data dependencies and future Linear ticket.
