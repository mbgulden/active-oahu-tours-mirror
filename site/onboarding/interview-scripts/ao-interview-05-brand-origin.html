🎙️ Expert Interview — Brand Story & Origin

Interview script for Michael Gulden — Active Oahu foundation pages
Interviewer: Hermes
Expert: Michael Gulden, Owner & Operator, Active Oahu — Kailua, Oahu (since 2023)
Use for: activeoahutours.com — 6 foundation pages
Target keywords: about active oahu, kailua kayak shop, best kayak rental oahu, active oahu reviews
Feeds: /about-active-oahu/, /why-choose-active-oahu/, /faq/, /reviews/, /kayak-safety-guide/ (enhancement), /meet-the-team/
Instructions

Instructions, Michael: This one's different — it's not about a location, it's about YOU. Record naturally. Tell stories. Be honest. The goal is to put a real face and voice behind the business — something KBA will NEVER do because their owner stays anonymous.

Section 1: The Origin Story

1. Why this business? Take me back to the beginning. What made you start Active Oahu? Was there a specific moment, a frustration, a gap you saw? What were you doing before?
2. Why Kailua? You could have set up anywhere on Oahu. Why 134B Hamakua Drive? What made you choose that specific location?
3. The day before your lease closed, you got that DLNR warning about the new Kaneohe Bay law. Describe that moment. What went through your head? How close did you come to walking away?
4. What's the hardest thing about running this business that customers never see? The 5 AM mornings, the equipment maintenance, the weather stress — what's the real cost?
5. Three years in, what's better than you expected? What surprised you about this life?

Section 2: Why Active Oahu — The Honest Pitch

6. If someone's choosing between Active Oahu and Kailua Beach Adventures, what's the honest difference? Not the marketing version — the real difference in experience.
7. Describe your ideal customer. Who really "gets" what you're offering? Who comes back year after year?
8. What's the worst-fit customer? The person who should probably book with someone else. Be honest — this builds trust.
9. The self-serve model — you're moving toward customers picking up gear and going on their own. Why? What's better about this vs. the traditional tour model?
10. What does "Active Oahu" mean to you? Beyond the business name — what's the philosophy?

Section 3: Customers & Community

11. Tell me about your favorite customer story. Someone who came back, someone who had a transformative experience, someone who became a friend. What happened?
12. What's the best compliment you've ever received? Something a customer said that stuck with you.
13. Do you have reviews that make you proud? Specific ones — what did they say that captured exactly what you're trying to do?
14. What's your relationship with the Kailua community? Do locals use your shop? Do you know the neighbors? How do you fit into the town?
15. If a customer has a bad experience — weather cancels their trip, gear breaks, they're unhappy — how do you handle it? Walk me through a real example.

Section 4: The Vision

16. Five years from now, what does Active Oahu look like? More locations? Different services? Same shop, better systems?
17. If you could wave a magic wand and fix one thing about the kayak/rental industry on Oahu — regulation, competition, access — what would it be?
18. What do you want people to feel after a day with Active Oahu? Not just "that was fun" — the deeper thing. What's the feeling you're actually selling?
19. If someone's reading your website trying to decide whether to book — what's the ONE thing you want them to know about you that they won't find on any other rental site?

Section 5: Awards & Credibility

20. TripAdvisor Traveler's Choice Awards — you've won this for years (2015, 2017-2024). What does that actually mean? How do you earn it? Does it matter to customers?
21. "People Love Us on Yelp" 2025 — what's the story there? What kind of reviews earned that?
22. Beyond awards, what should someone look at to know you're legit? Insurance? Safety certifications? Years in business? Local knowledge? What actually matters?
