🎙️ Expert Interview — Kaneohe Bay & The Sandbar (Ahu o Laka)

Interview script for Michael Gulden — Active Oahu Kaneohe Sandbar content cluster
Interviewer: Hermes
Expert: Michael Gulden, Owner & Operator, Active Oahu — Kailua, Oahu
Use for: activeoahutours.com — 6 pages in the Kaneohe Sandbar content cluster
Target keywords: kaneohe sandbar kayak, ahu o laka, kaneohe bay kayaking
Feeds: kaneohe-sandbar/, kaneohe-sandbar-tide-calendar, kaneohe-marine-life, kayak-anchor-guide, kaneohe-bay-vs-kailua, kaneohe-sandbar-rental
Instructions

Instructions, Michael: Same format — record naturally, no script-reading. If you're driving past Kaneohe Bay, pull over and record from Heʻeia Kea Pier for bonus authenticity.

Section 1: The Experience

1. Describe the first time you paddled out to the Kaneohe Sandbar. What did you expect vs what you actually experienced? The moment the water goes from deep to waist-deep to ankle-deep — what's that like?
2. For someone who's never heard of it: what IS the Kaneohe Sandbar? Why does it exist? What makes it one of Oahu's most unique natural features?
3. On weekends, it gets crowded — boats anchor up, people wade around. Is it a party? A family scene? Describe the vibe at peak sandbar.
4. What's the biggest mistake people make going to the sandbar? (Let me guess — wrong tide timing?)
5. What's the BEST moment at the sandbar? The hour, the tide level, the light — paint that picture.

Section 2: Practical How-To

6. Walk me through the paddle from Heʻeia Kea Pier out to the sandbar. Distance, time, what you see along the way. Is it harder or easier than people expect?
7. Tides — this is THE thing. Explain exactly how to read the tide chart for the sandbar. What tide level is ideal? What happens at high tide? What tide level means "don't bother"?
8. Anchoring a kayak at the sandbar — why do you need an anchor and how do you set it up? What happens if you DON'T anchor?
9. What marine life do people encounter? Turtles? Rays? What's in the surrounding coral heads?
10. Sun exposure — there's NO shade out there. How do people handle this? What do you recommend they bring?

Section 3: Comparisons & Context

11. Kaneohe Bay vs Kailua Bay — honest comparison for kayaking. Which has better conditions? Which is better for beginners? Where do locals actually prefer?
12. Kaneohe Sandbar vs Mokulua Islands — if someone can only do one, which should they pick and why?
13. The sandbar is a Marine Life Conservation District — what does that mean? What can't people do? Why is it protected?
14. What's the cultural significance of Ahu o Laka? Is there Hawaiian history here beyond "floating beach party"?
15. Heʻeia Kea Pier — is this really the best launch? Why not somewhere else? What's the deal with parking there?

Section 4: Safety & Conditions

16. What conditions make Kaneohe Bay dangerous? Wind? Reef? Boat traffic? What are the real risks?
17. Ever had a close call at the sandbar? A customer who got into trouble? What happened?
18. Kaneohe Bay is HUGE — what happens if someone gets tired, disoriented, or blown off course?
19. The sun + no shade + reflection off water = dehydration machine. What's your hydration/sun protection protocol?
20. Any sketchy boat behavior out there? Do boaters respect kayakers?

📋 Delivery

Upload your audio/video to the Linear task. MP3, MP4, or voice memo.

15-20 minutes of answers = 6 pages worth of authentic content that KBA can NEVER produce because they don't operate here.
