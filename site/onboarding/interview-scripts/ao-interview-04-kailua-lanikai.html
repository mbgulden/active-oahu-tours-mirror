Kailua & Lanikai — Second-Position Interview Script

For Michael Gulden — Owner, Active Oahu Tours (est. 2023) | 21 Questions Across 5 Sections
Interview angle: OUT-QUALITY, don't out-muscle. Kailua Beach Adventures (KBA) owns positions #1–7 with generic Squarespace blogs. Michael's edge: he operates FROM Kailua, has a physical shop at 134B Hamakua Drive, original photography, and access to daily wind/tide/crowd data no remote blogger has.
Pages this interview feeds (8 total): Kailua Beach Guide, Lanikai vs Kailua Beach, Kayaking to the Mokes: 12 Things Guidebooks Get Wrong, Lanikai Pillbox Hike Parking, Kailua Beach Park Facilities Guide, Best Time to Kayak Kailua, Where to Eat in Kailua, The Mokulua Islands Guide
Instructions

Instructions for Michael: Record your answers as audio. Speak naturally — the raw, first-person voice IS the competitive moat. Don't polish. Don't read from notes. If a question overlaps with something you already covered, skip it. The more stories and specific numbers you drop, the better.

Section 1: Kailua Beach — Ground Truth From the Shop Floor

Feeds: Kailua Beach Guide, Facilities Guide

Q1. Your shop is at 134B Hamakua Drive. Walk me through what a first-time visitor gets wrong about Kailua Beach before they even arrive. What's the thing guidebooks never mention that you see trip up tourists every single day?
Q2. Kailua Beach Park's facilities — the bathrooms, showers, parking lot, pavilions — everyone talks about them but nobody gives actual detail. What's the real state of the facilities right now?
Q3. Every guidebook says "Kailua Beach is 2.5 miles of white sand" and moves on. As someone who's on this beach constantly, what are the distinct zones or sections of Kailua Beach that tourists should know about?
Q4. What's the parking situation at Kailua Beach Park at 7am vs 9am vs noon on a Saturday? Give me the honest, unvarnished version — including how bad the theft problem actually is and where you personally tell your customers to park.

Section 2: Lanikai vs Kailua — The Honest Local Comparison

Feeds: Lanikai vs Kailua Beach, Pillbox Hike Parking

Q5. Every travel blog frames this as "Lanikai is prettier, Kailua is more convenient." As someone who operates from Kailua, what's your honest, unvarnished comparison of these two beaches? When is Lanikai actually overrated?
Q6. Lanikai Beach has ZERO public parking. Zero bathrooms. Zero showers. Yet tourists flock there. What's the actual impact of this?
Q7. The Lanikai Pillbox Hike (Ka'iwa Ridge Trail) — parking is the #1 nightmare and nobody writes about it well. Walk me through the exact, legal parking strategy.
Q8. If you had a friend visiting and they asked "Lanikai or Kailua today?" — what specific factors make you pick one over the other on any given day?
Q9. KBA's content and most guidebooks talk about Lanikai like it's a secret paradise. What's the reality when it's crowded? Paint me the picture of Lanikai on a peak Saturday.

Section 3: Kayaking to the Mokulua Islands — The Operator's Reality

Feeds: Kayaking to the Mokes, Mokulua Islands Guide

Q10. Kayaking to the Mokes — this is THE iconic Kailua kayak trip, but most guidebook content is written by people who've done it maybe once. What are the 3–4 things guidebooks consistently get wrong about the crossing?
Q11. Landing permits for the Mokes are strictly capped by the state. Most tourists don't know this exists. Explain the permit situation.
Q12. Wind and tide completely change the Mokes crossing. Based on your daily experience, what's the specific wind speed threshold where you tell customers "not today"?
Q13. The wildlife on the Mokulua Islands — wedge-tailed shearwaters nest there. What are the actual nesting zones and how to visit respectfully?
Q14. KBA talks about the Mokes crossing as a "leisurely 30–45 minute paddle." Based on your customers' actual experiences, what's the real range of crossing times?

Section 4: Wind, Tide & the Kayak Operator's Calendar

Feeds: Best Time to Kayak Kailua, Wind/Tide/Crowd Calendar

Q15. Kailua's trade winds are a daily reality for kayakers. Walk me through the typical wind pattern — time of day, windiest months, and the "go/no-go" wind speed.
Q16. The best time to kayak Kailua — most blogs say "morning." Give me the nuanced reality month by month.
Q17. Tide matters for Kailua kayaking in ways tourists never think about — launching, landing on the Mokes, navigating the reef.
Q18. Crowd patterns — you see the ebb and flow from your shop. When is Kailua Beach actually quiet?

Section 5: After the Beach — Food, Logistics & The Kailua Day

Feeds: Where to Eat in Kailua After Your Kayak Trip

Q19. After a kayak trip, people are hungry, sandy, and probably sunburned. What are your personal restaurant recommendations in Kailua town?
Q20. The logistics of a Kailua beach day that nobody writes about — where do you get ice, nearest grocery store, what if gear gets stolen?
Q21. If someone has ONE day in Kailua and wants to kayak, do the pillbox hike, and eat well — in that order — what's the specific, hour-by-hour itinerary you'd build for them?
