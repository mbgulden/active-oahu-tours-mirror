🎙️ Expert Interview — Kualoa & Chinaman's Hat (Mokoliʻi Island)

Interview script for Michael Gulden — Active Oahu content cluster
Interviewer: Hermes
Expert: Michael Gulden, Owner & Operator, Active Oahu — operating in Kailua since 2023
Use for: activeoahutours.com — 8 pages in the Chinaman's Hat content cluster
Target keywords: chinamans hat kayak, mokolii island, kualoa kayak, chinamans hat vs mokes
Feeds: chinamans-hat/, mokolii/, kualoa-bay-guide, chinamans-hat-tide-guide, chinamans-hat-packing-list, chinamans-hat-vs-mokes, jurassic-park-kayak, chinamans-hat-rental
Instructions

Instructions, Michael: Record this as audio or video. Answer naturally — like you're telling a friend over a beer. No script-reading, no formal tone. Drive to Kualoa and record from the beach if you want. We'll pull quotes, stories, and facts into web pages, social posts, and video shorts.

Section 1: The Paddle Itself (experiential)

1. Describe paddling out toward Chinaman's Hat from Kualoa Beach Park for the first time. What do you see? What's the water like under your kayak? What surprises people about this paddle vs what they expected?
2. "It's only 500 yards" — is that actually true? Walk me through the exact paddle distance, time, and what the reef shelf feels like underneath. Is it really never deeper than waist-deep?
3. What's the biggest mistake people make on this paddle? The one thing you wish every single renter knew before they pushed off?
4. On a scale of "my grandma could do this" to "only for experienced kayakers," where does Chinaman's Hat actually land? Be honest.
5. What's the moment that makes this trip special? Is it the paddle out, landing on the island, or the summit? Describe that peak moment.

Section 2: Practical Details (maps to how-to pages)

6. Walk me through the ENTIRE experience from the customer's perspective — from the moment they meet you at the shop to the moment they return. What happens at each step?
7. The hike to the summit — people say it's steep and rocky. How sketchy is it actually? What shoes do you need? Has anyone ever slipped or gotten hurt?
8. Tides. This is the thing people mess up. Explain Chinaman's Hat tides like I'm 12 — when can you go, when should you absolutely NOT go, and how do you read the tide chart?
9. What gear do YOU personally use for this trip? Not the rental minimum — what do you bring when you go for fun?
10. Kualoa Regional Park — where exactly do you park? Where's the best launch spot? What facilities are there (bathrooms, showers, shade)?

Section 3: Comparisons & Deep Knowledge

11. Chinaman's Hat vs Mokulua Islands (The Mokes) — honest comparison. Which is better for a first-timer? Which has the better view? Which would you recommend and why?
12. The Hawaiian legend of Mokoliʻi — tell it. The moʻo, Hiʻiaka, the dragon's tail. How do you tell this story to visitors?
13. Kualoa Ranch is RIGHT there — Jurassic Park, Lost, Kong: Skull Island were filmed with this mountain range as backdrop. Do visitors even realize they're kayaking through a movie set? What's the coolest filming fact?
14. What wildlife do people actually see? Turtles? Rays? What's in the water between Kualoa and the island?
15. Every guidebook mentions Chinaman's Hat. What's something NO guidebook mentions? A secret, a tip, a detail only someone who's done this 100 times would know?

Section 4: Safety & Conditions

16. What conditions make you say "absolutely not today"? Wind direction, swell, tide? What are the real red flags?
17. Has anything dangerous or scary ever happened during a Chinaman's Hat trip? Be honest — what's the actual risk?
18. The reef — people worry about cutting their feet. What's the reality? Do you need water shoes?
19. If someone capsizes between Kualoa and the island (it happens), what do they do? Step-by-step.
20. Cell service out there? What happens if someone gets in trouble?

Section 5: Stories & Human Touch

21. Tell me about your most memorable Chinaman's Hat customer. What made that trip special?
22. Funniest thing that's ever happened on this paddle. A tourist doing something ridiculous, a wildlife encounter, a gear mishap?
23. What's the feeling when someone summits Mokoliʻi for the first time? Describe the view — what do you see in every direction?
24. If someone has ONE afternoon on Oahu and has to choose between Chinaman's Hat and any other kayak trip — make the case for Chinaman's Hat. Why this one?
25. Five years from now, what do you want the Chinaman's Hat experience to look like through Active Oahu? What's the vision?

📋 Delivery

Upload your audio/video to the Linear task. Format: MP3, MP4, or voice memo.

Answer as many questions as you want in one session. Even 15 minutes of answers gets us 80% of the content.

After upload: Swarm transcribes → extracts quotes → generates web pages, social posts, and schema markup. One recording = 8 pages + 10 social posts.
