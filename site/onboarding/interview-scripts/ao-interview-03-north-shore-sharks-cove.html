🎙️ Expert Interview — North Shore & Sharks Cove

Interview script for Michael Gulden — Active Oahu North Shore content cluster
Interviewer: Hermes
Expert: Michael Gulden, Owner & Operator, Active Oahu — Kailua, Oahu
Use for: activeoahutours.com — 6 North Shore content cluster pages
Format: Audio-recorded responses (~45–60 min total)

Section 1 — The Lay of the Land: North Shore Snorkeling Overview

Feeds: North Shore Snorkeling: When, Where & How · Waimea Bay to Sharks Cove: North Shore Water Activities Map

Q1. When someone walks into your Kailua shop and says "I want to snorkel the North Shore" — what's the very first thing you tell them? Walk me through the conversation.
Q2. Most visitors picture the North Shore as one big beach. Map it out for us — what are the actual snorkel spots between, say, Haleʻiwa and Sunset Beach, and how different are they from each other?
Q3. You rent gear from Kailua and send people 45 minutes up the coast. What are the biggest surprises or misunderstandings first-timers have about North Shore snorkeling versus what they expected from a guidebook?
Q4. Seasonality is huge here. Break down what North Shore snorkeling looks like in summer (May–September) versus winter (October–April) — and when is it actually not snorkelable?
Q5. If someone has exactly one morning to snorkel the North Shore, what's your personal itinerary — which spot, what time, and what's the one thing they should NOT skip?

Section 2 — Sharks Cove: The Insider's Playbook

Feeds: Sharks Cove: The Complete Self-Guided Snorkel Guide · Sharks Cove Snorkel Gear Rentals

Q6. Sharks Cove is the name everyone searches — but when someone actually arrives there for the first time, what do they see that no photo or YouTube video captures? Describe the entry, the rocks, the vibe.
Q7. There's a right way and a wrong way to enter the water at Sharks Cove. Walk me through the mistake you see tourists make over and over, and how you coach your rental customers to do it safely.
Q8. Once you're in the water, where are the zones? Where do the turtles hang, where's the best coral, and where should people absolutely not go?
Q9. You're sending people with your rental masks and fins — what gear actually matters at Sharks Cove specifically? Fins or no fins? Full-face mask or traditional? What do you stock and why?
Q10. Sharks Cove gets compared to Hanauma Bay constantly. When a customer asks you "Should I do Sharks Cove or Hanauma Bay?" — what's the real answer you give them, off the record?

Section 3 — Marine Life: What's Actually Down There

Feeds: North Shore Marine Life: Turtles, Fish & Seasonal Visitors · Sharks Cove: The Complete Self-Guided Snorkel Guide

Q11. Everyone wants to see sea turtles. Where on the North Shore are your customers actually seeing them most consistently — and what's the real behavior you coach people on so they don't get fined or hurt a turtle?
Q12. Beyond turtles, what's the marine life that surprises people at Sharks Cove and Waimea Bay — the stuff they come back raving about that they didn't expect to see?
Q13. Seasonal visitors — tell me about the whales, the spinner dolphins, the manta rays. What time of year do your customers report seeing each of these, and from where?
Q14. Any marine life encounters that went sideways? Either a customer got too close to something they shouldn't have, or something unexpected showed up. What happened?

Section 4 — Safety, Conditions & The Stuff Guidebooks Skip

Feeds: All 6 pages, especially Sharks Cove: The Complete Self-Guided Snorkel Guide · North Shore Snorkeling: When, Where & How

Q15. You've been renting gear and sending people to the North Shore for three years. What are the real safety concerns at Sharks Cove that TripAdvisor and blog posts don't mention?
Q16. Currents, swell, surge — break those terms down for someone who's never snorkeled in Hawaii. How do you teach a rental customer to read the water before they even get in?
Q17. Sun protection is a topic nobody talks about honestly. What sunscreen rules are actually enforced on the North Shore now, and what do you tell people who show up with a bottle of spray sunscreen from Target?
Q18. Sharks Cove is named Sharks Cove. Address the elephant in the room — are there sharks, has anyone seen one while snorkeling there, and what's the actual risk level?

Section 5 — The Business Side & Real Stories

Feeds: Sharks Cove Snorkel Gear Rentals · Sharks Cove vs Hanauma Bay · All pages (story snippets)

Q19. You run a gear rental business in Kailua, not on the North Shore. Why should someone rent from you and drive up, versus finding a rental shop closer to Sharks Cove? What's the value proposition?
Q20. What's the funniest or most memorable customer story from someone who rented your gear and went to the North Shore — the kind of story you tell at parties?
Q21. If the North Shore had a "locals only" secret — a tip, a timing, a spot that only people who live on Oahu know — what's one piece of advice you'd share that you wouldn't find in any guidebook?
Q22. Three years in — what's changed about North Shore snorkeling since you started Active Oahu? More crowded? Different conditions? New rules? And what do you think it'll look like in five years?
