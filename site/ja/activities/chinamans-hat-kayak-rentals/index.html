How It Works

  1. Book online — Select your date and time on our real-time availability calendar
  2. Check in at our Kailua shop — 134B Hamakua Dr, Kailua. We'll help you load the kayak onto your car with foam pads and straps

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to make a reservation?

Yes, we recommend booking ahead, especially in summer and on weekends. Use our real-time calendar to check availability and secure your spot.

What if I've never kayaked before?

No problem. The paddle to Chinaman's Hat is one of the safest on Oahu, and we'll give you a thorough lesson before you head out. The water is shallow, protected by a reef, and the crossing is only 500 yards.

Can I take the kayak somewhere else?

Your rental is for the Chinaman's Hat route, but we also rent kayaks for Kailua Bay and the Mokes. Let us know what you're planning and we'll set you up.

What if the weather turns bad?

We monitor conditions closely. If we need to cancel due to high winds or storms, we'll let you know as soon as possible and offer a full refund or re-schedule.
