Beach gear rentals from our shop or delivery to your private address

Kayak and Beach Gear Rental Overview

We provide rental equipment so you can experience the best kayaking locations on the island. We also provide custom delivery for vacation rental addresses. We make kayaking in Oahu a breeze.

Our Top Kayak Rental Experiences

You can select any of these locations under the 4 hour, or full day rental options on our online booking calendar. All of these locations offer great year-round kayaking:

  • Mokoliʻi Island - Chinaman's Hat, near Kualoa Regional Park
    Kayak and dry bag rentals.

  • Kaneohe Sandbar - Ahu‘O Laka, Kaneohe's Sunken Island
    Kayak, dry bag, and snorkel gear rentals.

  • Kahana River - Ahupuaʻa ʻO Kahana State Park
    Kayak, standup paddleboard, and dry bag rentals.

  • Kaneohe Bay - coral reef underwater islands
    Kayak, dry bag, and snorkel gear rentals.

  • Kailua - Mokulua Islands and Popoia Island
    Kayak and dry bag rentals

Our Top Beach Gear Experiences

With calm water in the summer and big waves on north shore in the winter, It's always a good time to relax on the beach.

  • Vacation rentals - all over the island
    Year-round - Get gear delivered to your doorstep.

  • Kuilima Cove - near Turtle Bay Resort
    Year-round - Beach chairs, umbrella, dry bag, and snorkel gear.

  • Kawela Bay - a protected cove on North Shore
    Year-round - Standup paddleboard, beach chairs, umbrella, dry bag, boogie board, and snorkel gear.

  • Sunset Beach and Waimea Bay - amazing beaches year-round
    Summer (April-October) - Standup paddleboard, beach chairs, umbrella, dry bag, boogie board, and snorkel gear.
    Winter(November to March) - Beach chairs, umbrella, portable cooler.

  • Kailua Beach - family friendly
    Year-round - Standup paddleboard, beach chairs, umbrella, dry bag, boogie board, and snorkel gear.

  • Lani Kai - a calm relaxing picutresque beach
    Year-round - Standup paddleboard, beach chairs, umbrella, dry bag, boogie board, and snorkel gear.

  • Kalama - approachable shorebreak for boogie boarding
    Year-round - Standup paddleboard, beach chairs, umbrella, dry bag, boogie board, and snorkel gear.

Our Gear Rental Options

Now that you've read how rental work and our most popular rental equipment locations and combinations, Let's talk about how long you'll need the gear to have a great Oahu beach day.

We offer 3 main options with our rental service:

Kayak rentals out at the Kaneohe Sandbar

The "full day" rental time-period is between 8am to 5pm the same day (not a 24 hour period).

There is a $76 minimum charge for booking our rental delivery service.. This includes the transportation costs, delivery and pickup; so be sure to rent more than $76 worth of equipment to get your money's worth.

Our standard rental delivery service area is between Waimea Bay and Kaneohe Bay (North & East Sides of the island). We can accommodate larger orders on other parts of the island depending on availability.

Booking questions you should be prepared to answer:

  • What equipment do you want to rent?
    Kayaks are our most popular item, but you can also select standup paddleboards, beach chairs and more.
    For snorkel fin & mask rentals please include sizes in the notes section: (Kids, SM, M, L or XL)

  • Where would you like to use your kayak or beach rental gear?
    Our most popular use of rental kayaks are at Mokolii (Chinaman's Hat) and the Kahana Valley River
    Each of those locations are 30-40 minutes from our storefront. Another option is to kayak in Kaneohe Bay where the boat harbor is only 20 minutes away.

  • When will you come to the shop and pickup/drop off the rentals?
    Usually, we work with 4 hour time blocks outlined on our calendar, but for all day rentals the normal pickup times are 12, 3pm and 5pm
