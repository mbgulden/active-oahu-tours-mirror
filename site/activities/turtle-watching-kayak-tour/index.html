Turtle Watching Kayak Tour — Paddle with Honu on Oahu

See Hawaiian Green Sea Turtles in Their Natural Habitat

Hawaiian green sea turtles — honu in ʻōlelo Hawaiʻi — are one of the most beloved creatures in these islands. And there's no better way to see them than from a kayak on Kailua Bay. Our turtle watching kayak tour takes you to the feeding grounds and resting areas where honu are most frequently spotted, all while respecting their space and natural behavior.

As we paddle the calm waters of Kailua Bay, your guide will point out turtles surfacing for air, grazing on seagrass beds, and occasionally basking on sandy patches near the reef. We see them on nearly every tour — and the looks on our guests' faces when a massive honu glides under their kayak never gets old.

We launch from Kailua Beach, paddling toward areas where seagrass beds and reef structures attract turtles. Unlike crowded snorkel spots where turtles are surrounded by swimmers, our kayaks give you a quiet, elevated view. Turtles are less disturbed by kayaks than by swimmers, so you'll see more natural behavior — feeding, surfacing, even mating rituals during the right season.

Our guides are knowledgeable about honu conservation and biology. We follow strict guidelines: maintaining distance, never chasing or cornering turtles, and educating guests about responsible wildlife viewing. This isn't just a tour — it's a chance to connect with one of Hawaiʻi's most sacred animals in a way that honors their space.

Perfect for families, photographers, and anyone who loves wildlife. Kids as young as 8 are welcome in tandem kayaks with an adult. No kayaking experience needed.

Where We Paddle — Turtle Hotspots on Kailua Bay

Kailua Bay is one of the most reliable places on Oahu's windward coast to spot honu. The bay's shallow seagrass beds provide an ideal feeding habitat, and the protected waters mean turtles can rest without fighting strong currents. Our guides have been paddling these waters daily for years — we know exactly which areas the turtles favor at different times of day and tide levels.

We typically paddle toward the southern end of Kailua Beach, near the channel markers where seagrass grows thickest. On many tours, we also drift near the edge of the reef flat, where turtles cruise the drop-off looking for algae. If conditions allow, we'll paddle toward Flat Island (Popoia) where turtles congregate around the island's rocky edges.

Beyond honu, keep an eye out for spinner dolphins, eagle rays, and humuhumunukunukuapuaʻa (Hawaiian reef triggerfish) in the crystal-clear shallows. The bay is alive with marine life, and every tour is different.

Responsible Turtle Watching

Honu are protected under Hawaiʻi state law and the Endangered Species Act. Federal guidelines require maintaining at least 10 feet of distance from turtles. From a kayak, this is easy — you get a perfect view without getting close enough to disturb them. Your guide will explain the dos and don'ts before launch and monitor wildlife behavior throughout the tour.

We never chase turtles, block their path, or corner them against the shore. If a turtle approaches your kayak (which happens more often than you'd think), enjoy the moment — quietly and without sudden movements. A honu surfacing two feet from your kayak is a memory you'll carry forever.

Best Season for Turtle Watching

Honu are present year-round in Kailua Bay, but the best viewing is during summer months (May to October) when water clarity is highest and turtles are more active. Winter storms can stir up sediment, reducing visibility. Morning tours typically offer the calmest conditions and most active turtle feeding.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often do you see turtles?

Turtles are spotted on over 90% of our tours. Because we choose our paddle route based on where the turtles have been feeding recently, we have excellent success rates. We can't guarantee wildlife sightings, but our guides know this bay intimately.

Can I swim with the turtles?

This is a kayak-based tour, not a snorkel tour. However, we often pause to watch turtles from the kayak, which gives you a better vantage point than swimming. If you want to snorkel with turtles, check out our Kayak + Snorkel + Hike combo tour.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Absolutely. The calm waters of Kailua Bay are perfect for first-time kayakers, and our guides provide instruction before you launch. The turtle areas are close to shore, so the paddling distance is manageable for all fitness levels.

What if it rains?

Light rain won't cancel the tour — turtles don't mind a little water! If conditions are unsafe, we reschedule or offer a full refund. We make the call by 7 AM for morning tours and 12 PM for afternoon tours.
