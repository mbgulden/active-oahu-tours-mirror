Kaneohe Sandbar Kayak Rentals

By Michael Gulden, Owner & Operator, Active Oahu — Kailua, Oahu

Ready to paddle to Kaneohe Sandbar? We've got your gear. Rent a kayak from our Kailua storefront and we'll set you up with everything you need for a perfect day at Ahu O Laka.

  • $82 – Visitor
  • $70 – Kama'aina / Military

What's Included

  • Tandem kayak with seat backs
  • Life vests (PFDs)
  • Dry bag
  • Kayak anchor (for the sandbar)
  • Pads, straps, and on-site instruction
