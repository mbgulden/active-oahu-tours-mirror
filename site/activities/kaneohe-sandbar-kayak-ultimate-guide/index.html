Kaneohe Sandbar Kayak: The Ultimate Guide

By Michael Gulden, Owner & Operator, Active Oahu — Kailua, Oahu

Kaneohe Sandbar (Ahu O Laka) is one of Oahu's most unique kayaking destinations — a submerged sandbar that emerges at low tide, creating a shallow, turquoise playground in the middle of Kaneohe Bay. This guide covers everything you need to plan your trip.

Getting There

The best launch points are He'eia Kea Boat Harbor and Kualoa Regional Park. The paddle takes 20-40 minutes depending on wind and tide. A rising tide gives you the most time on the sandbar.

What to Expect

  • Shallow, calm water perfect for all skill levels
  • Stunning views of the Koolau mountain range
  • Coconut Island (Moku O Lo'e) and the Mokes on the horizon
  • Warm, crystal-clear water to wade in
