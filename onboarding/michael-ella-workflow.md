# Michael's Guide: Managing Ella's Workflow

This is your tactical playbook for getting the best work out of Ella without burning her out. Keep this handy.

---

## Core Principle: Ella Is Not a Content Machine

Ella's value is her **local knowledge and judgment**, not her output speed. She is the human in the loop who catches what AI misses. Treat her energy as a finite resource — because it is.

---

## Assignment Protocol

### Batch Size: 5 Tasks Max

Assign work in batches of exactly 5 tasks. Never more. When she finishes a batch, you can assign the next one. This keeps her momentum up without the paralysis of seeing 30 open tasks.

### Give Clear Scope

Every batch should have a clear theme or boundary so she knows what "done" looks like. Examples:

- **Good:** "Here are 5 interview tasks for North Shore topics — all voice memos, ~10 min each."
- **Good:** "Review these 5 AI-drafted articles against the checklist I attached."
- **Bad:** "Here's a bunch of stuff, do whatever."

The interview tasks (GRO-142 through GRO-147) cover 6 core Active Oahu content topics. Start her on the ones she knows intimately to build confidence:

## The Weekly 10-Minute Sync

Every week, hop on a 10-minute call or voice message exchange. The agenda:

1. **Tell her what she caught that was valuable.** "Hey, in that Lanikai piece you flagged that the parking situation changed — that saved us from publishing bad info. Thank you." This reinforces that her judgment matters and she's not just a rubber stamp.
2. **Preview the next batch.** Give her a heads-up on what's coming so nothing is a surprise.
3. **Ask how it's going.** Listen for fatigue signals (see below).

That's it. Don't make it longer. She'll start dreading it.

---

## Ella's Sweet Spot

Ella shines when she's:

- **Reviewing completed work against a clear checklist** — give her 3-5 specific things to look for ("check all directions, verify hours of operation, confirm parking info")
- **Answering concrete, local-knowledge questions** via voice memo — she can do this in 5-10 minutes per task from her couch
- **Catching AI mistakes** — wrong street names, outdated closures, things that "just feel off" to a local

She struggles when asked to:

- Create content from scratch (that's the AI's job)
- Do open-ended research ("find me 20 things to do in Kailua")
- Work without a clear deliverable or checklist

**Rule of thumb:** If the task instruction is longer than 3 sentences, it's probably too open-ended for her. Tighten the scope.

---

## Signs She's Overwhelmed

Watch for these signals and intervene early:

1. **Comments get shorter and vaguer.** "Looks fine" on something that clearly needed notes = fatigue.
2. **Response time drops off a cliff.** If she normally turns around a batch in 2 days and suddenly it's been 5, check in.
3. **She stops asking clarifying questions.** An engaged Ella asks follow-ups. Silence = either confused or checked out.
4. **Tasks pile up in "In Progress."** She's stuck and hasn't said so.
5. **She says "I'll get to it soon" more than twice on the same batch.**

When you see these: reduce batch size to 3. Explicitly tell her it's okay to go slower. Ask if she wants to skip the review tasks and just do voice memos for a week.

---

## Communication Cadence

- **Assignments:** Post in Linear as new tasks. She checks Linear, not her email.
- **Check-ins:** Your weekly 10-min sync. Don't DM her between syncs unless something is genuinely urgent.
- **Urgent stuff:** DM with a clear subject line — "Need answer on Mokulua draft by Thursday" — not "hey quick question."
- **Praise:** Publicly in task comments so the AI logs it. "Ella caught a great detail here — using this version." It builds trust in the human-AI loop.

---

## The AI Handoff Loop

Make sure you understand this so you can unblock her if needed:

1. You assign task → AI generates initial draft or questions
2. Ella comments → AI reads and acts
3. AI posts revised version → Ella reviews
4. Loop continues until she marks it done

If the loop stalls, the most common causes are:
- Ella's comment was too vague for the AI to parse (coach her on specificity — point her to the examples in her onboarding guide)
- The AI didn't trigger (ping the system or reassign)
- Ella is waiting on something she doesn't realize she needs to provide
